The Electric Outlet is a personal research notebook exploring the physical bottlenecks of technology, hardware infrastructure, energy markets, and capital strategy.

As systems grow more complex, performance is increasingly constrained by the real world—power grid interconnections, wide-area network latency, thermal dynamics, and manufacturing supply chains. This platform serves as a space to document field notes, operational breakdowns, and evolving theses.

📈 Capital Allocation & Strategy Evaluating early-stage venture thesis formation, infrastructure economics, and scaling bottlenecks.
🌐 Distributed Systems & Networking Exploring optical switch fabrics, Wide-Area Network (WAN) routing, and network Points-of-Presence (PoPs).
